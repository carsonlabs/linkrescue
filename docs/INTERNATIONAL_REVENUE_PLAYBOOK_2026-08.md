# LinkRescue international revenue playbook

**Status:** founder-pricing plan; no public promise, send, spend, deployment, or billing action is authorised by this document.
**Market:** English-speaking affiliate publishers and boutique SEO/content agencies, sold in USD.
**Business thesis:** sell a verified link-integrity outcome first; use self-serve monitoring only after its database and customer journey are restored.

## 1. The business

LinkRescue is an **Affiliate Revenue Recovery Desk**. It does not claim to generate revenue. It helps a publisher or agency find affiliate links that are broken, misdirected, or lose tracking parameters, then gives the team a clear repair queue.

The defensible proof point is LinkRescue's June 2026 research scan: 50 established affiliate sites, 683 pages, and 6,550 checked outbound links. Of the checked links, 5.8% were visibly broken and 9.1% had an attribution failure. Rates apply only to links checked within the stated crawl budget; bot-blocked links were reported separately.

### Ideal first buyer

| Priority | Buyer | Qualifying signal | Why they buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Boutique SEO/content agency | Manages 5+ content sites with affiliate links | One relationship can create recurring, multi-site revenue; reporting helps them retain clients. |
| 2 | Established affiliate publisher | 100+ monetised articles or 3+ affiliate networks | Old archive content makes maintenance difficult; a prioritised audit saves triage time. |
| Avoid for now | Hobby sites, brand-new blogs, coupon farms | Little archive or no disclosed affiliation | Low urgency, low ability to pay, and poor evidence of repeat need. |

### Positioning

**Category:** Affiliate link integrity service.
**One-line pitch:** *Know which affiliate links are failing before they disappear into your archive.*
**Differentiator:** Every reported issue includes the source page, affected destination, failure type, and the next repair decision—not an opaque "health score."

Do not say LinkRescue is the only tool, that it recovers a stated amount of money, or that it has protected a stated amount of revenue unless a dated, reviewable customer record supports the claim.

## 4. Delivery system

1. **Qualify:** confirm ownership/authorisation, approximate archive size, affiliate networks, and agency/client role.
2. **Scope:** state page cap, delivery date, output format, and what the crawler cannot verify (bot walls, JavaScript-only links, private destinations).
3. **Collect:** run the scan only against the authorised domain and preserve raw evidence.
4. **Verify:** agent classifies; a human reviews material issues and low-confidence results.
5. **Deliver:** share the evidence report and repair queue. Do not edit links or email the customer's clients.
6. **Convert:** offer monitoring only after the customer has seen the evidence and accepted the terms.
7. **Learn:** record actual audit time, scan cost, report quality, close reason, churn reason, and any customer-approved outcome. Only then create a case study.

### Minimum report structure

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Source page and anchor text | Lets the customer locate the affected content quickly. |
| Original URL and final destination | Establishes the redirect-chain evidence. |
| Issue type and confidence | Distinguishes confirmed breakage from a bot wall or ambiguous redirect. |
| Suggested repair action | Gives the customer a practical next step without touching their site. |
| Priority rationale | Uses traffic/business inputs supplied by the customer; never invents financial impact. |

## 5. Finance and measurement

### Planning case — not a forecast

| Proof milestone | Gross cash or recurring value before fees/tax | What it proves |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 Recovery Sprint | $499 cash | Someone will pay for the audit outcome. |
| 3 Recovery Sprints | $1,497 cash | The delivery can be repeated and costed. |
| 3 Monitoring Desk sites | $447 MRR | A recurring publisher service may exist. |
| 1 Agency Desk | $499 MRR | A multi-site recurring buyer may exist. |
| 4 Agency Desks | $1,996 MRR | A small, repeatable agency motion may exist; still not a profitability claim. |

Track gross receipts separately from: payment processing, tax reserve, scan/LLM spend, infrastructure, refunds, and operator time. Do not make a margin claim until five delivered customers have a complete cost record. Confirm tax registration, invoicing, privacy, and cross-border obligations with a qualified accountant/lawyer before taking international payments.

### Funnel scoreboard

| Leading measure | First 30-day target | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualified agencies/publishers with an approved contact path | 20 | Count only real, relevant prospects—not scraped volume. |
| Permissioned Leak Snapshots delivered | 10 | Every snapshot must include underlying evidence. |
| Conversations / discovery calls | 5 | Capture objection and buyer language verbatim. |
| Paid Recovery Sprints | 2 | A paid audit is the first real validation gate. |
| Monitoring conversion | 1 | Convert only after a delivered audit. |

**Stop rule:** after 25 genuinely relevant, lawful conversations or authorised snapshots, zero paid Sprints means stop building features and revise the buyer, offer, or price from the objections. Do not compensate with ads.

### Spend controls

- No paid ads until the snapshot-to-conversation rate and one paid audit are measured.
- No new tool, credit, or cloud subscription without Carson's approval and a monthly cap.
- Collect one-time Sprint payments before delivery; recurring plans require explicit customer authorisation.
- Keep a one-page payment/refund log from the first transaction.

## 7. OpenClaw: 24/7 operating role

OpenClaw is the internal operating layer, not an unattended sales bot. Its queue should do the continuous, low-risk work:

| Queue | Allowed | Must never do without Carson's explicit approval |
|---|---|---|
| Service health | Check uptime, environment presence, job failures, and spend; create an internal alert | Redeploy, rotate keys, create cloud resources, or change billing |
| Prospect research | Prepare a small, evidence-backed list and company notes | Contact anyone, scrape restricted data, or represent a relationship |
| Audit preparation | Build reports for domains with recorded authorisation; flag uncertainty | Scan unauthorised domains, alter a site, or claim financial outcomes |
| Messaging | Draft email, partner, social, and follow-up copy in an approval queue | Send email/DM/SMS, publish a post, or schedule public content |
| Revenue ops | Update internal pipeline and prepare invoice/check-out drafts | Charge a card, issue a refund, or alter customer records in production |
| Learning | Summarise objections, actual cost, and real outcome evidence weekly | Fabricate testimonials, case studies, or performance metrics |

**Cadence:** daily internal health/cost report; weekly pipeline and claim-audit report; monthly customer-outcome review. Reports should be short, action-labelled, and never create a draft pile that hides real work.

Until then, build internally, draft freely, and measure honestly.
